Metric definitions, date bases & data quality

    Weekly labels identify the Monday beginning each bucket; first/last buckets may be partial. Figures are observed activity, not official service-level assessments. Secure persistent export history is unavailable.

    Document Request Analytics

    Submission-period cohort, grouped by current status. Approval/rejection activity below uses actual recorded events during the period, independently of submission dates.

    Appointment Analytics

    Scheduled-date cohort grouped by current appointment status. Recorded reschedule events are actual history entries during the period and do not increase Total Appointments.

    Processing Intervals

    Cohort counts reflect current request status. Measured intervals require valid recorded timestamps with endpoints in the period; missing, invalid or reversed timestamps are excluded, never zero-filled.

    Release Analytics

    Actual handover-confirmed release snapshots dated in the period, regardless of submission date. Totals count requests; known copies sum recorded positive quantities only.

    Service Performance

    Submission cohort compared with recorded release events per document type. Sorting applies to this table only; no SLA or efficiency ranking is implied.

    Detailed Reports

    Requests submitted during the selected period. Lifecycle dates may be outside the period. Student identifiers and staff fields require separate reporting capabilities. Detailed search applies only to this table and detailed CSV.

    Your account can view aggregate reports only.

    Support Activity

    Authorized support scope only. Tickets use submission dates and current status. Resolutions use actual event dates. Document service, request status and program filters do not apply to this separate section. No private messages, notes or student identities are included.

    Export Operational Report

    CSV uses the applied reporting filters. Detailed exports include matching rows across all pages and the current detailed search/sort. No report files or sensitive export history are stored in browser-local storage.
